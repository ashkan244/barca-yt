## اسکریپت و تایم‌لاین

### 0:00 — قلاب (15 ثانیه)
**تصویر:** دو تخته‌ی کنار هم. چپ: مهره‌ی بی‌نام که فقط به توپ نگاه می‌کند (مخروط دید باریک قرمز). راست: مهره‌ی «8» با قوس‌های طلایی اسکن چپ و راست. توپ می‌رسد: چپی تحت فشار گیر می‌افتد، راستی با یک لمس می‌چرخد و پاس عمقی می‌دهد. **SFX:** whoosh روی چرخش. **موزیک:** هنوز نه.

> [PL-001] Most midfielders look at the ball. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-002] Pedri looks at everything else first. [pause 1.0]
> [PL-003] And that one habit is why he almost never loses it.

### 0:15 — وعده (30 ثانیه)
**تصویر:** سه کارت عادت که یکی‌یکی روی تخته می‌چسبند (SCAN · HALF-TURN · NEXT PASS)، سپس آیکون تمرین با تایمر «10:00». **موزیک:** تم PL شروع می‌شود (ریتمیک، کم).

> [PL-004] In the next ten minutes you'll learn three habits that make Pedri look like he has more time than everyone else. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-005] None of them need talent. All of them need practice. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-006] And at the end, I'll give you a ten-minute drill you can do tomorrow with four cones and one friend.

### 0:45 — چرا پدری؟ (60 ثانیه)
**تصویر:** تایم‌لاین گچی: تگسته (تنریف) ← لاس پالماس ← بارسلونا ۲۰۲۰ ← Golden Boy و Kopa ۲۰۲۱. نقشه‌ی جزایر قناری تا بارسلونا. کارت آمار: «52 appearances in his first season» (منبع: سایت باشگاه).

> [PL-007] Pedri grew up in Tegueste, a small town in Tenerife, and played for Las Palmas before Barcelona signed him. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-008] He arrived in 2020 as a teenager, and in his first season he played fifty-two times — more than anyone in the squad. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-009] By the end of 2021 he had won the Golden Boy and the Kopa Trophy, the two biggest awards for young players in the world. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-010] He isn't the fastest player on the pitch. He isn't the strongest. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-011] What he does better than almost anyone is decide early. And deciding early is a skill you can copy.
> [PL-011a] And at Euro 2020, still only eighteen, he was named the Young Player of the Tournament.

### 1:45 — عادت ۱: قبل از دریافت، اسکن کن (2:45)
**صحنه‌ها:**
1. (1:45) تخته از بالا: پدری در مرکز، فلش پاس از مدافع به سمت او. ساعت کوچک در گوشه: «1.5 s».
2. (2:05) فریز. قوس اسکن چپ (طلایی، کشیده‌شدن گچ)، قوس اسکن راست. شمارنده‌ی «SCAN 1 · SCAN 2». نمایش اینکه هر نگاه چه اطلاعاتی می‌دهد: حریف پشت سر، هم‌تیمی آزاد، فضای خالی.
3. (2:50) مقایسه‌ی دو نسخه‌ی همان لحظه: بدون اسکن (مهره بعد از دریافت مکث می‌کند، حریف می‌رسد، قرمز) / با اسکن (یک لمس و چرخش، سبز).
4. (3:30) نکته‌ی اجرایی: «شانه‌ی چپ، شانه‌ی راست، بعد توپ». سه آیکون سر.
5. (4:00) جمع‌بندی کوتاه.

> [PL-012] Habit one. Scan before you receive. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-013] Watch the moment the pass leaves the defender's foot. That's roughly a second and a half before the ball reaches the midfielder. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-014] An average player spends that time watching the ball travel. [pause 0.3]
> [PL-015] Pedri spends it looking away from the ball. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-016] First over one shoulder. Where's the nearest opponent? [pause 0.4]
> [PL-017] Then over the other. Where's the free teammate? Where's the space? [pause 0.6]
> [PL-018] By the time the ball arrives, the decision is already made. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-019] Here's the same moment twice. [pause 0.3]
> [PL-020] Without the scan, the player receives, looks up, and only then sees the opponent closing in. Half a second too late. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-021] With the scan, he already knows the pressure is coming from his left. So he takes his first touch to the right — and he's gone. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-022] The rule is simple enough to remember in a match: [pause 0.3]
> [PL-023] Left shoulder. Right shoulder. Then the ball. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-024] Coaches call this scanning, and the best midfielders in the world do it constantly — not once, but every few seconds, even when the ball is far away. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-025] Start with one rule: never receive a pass without having looked away from it at least once. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-025a] The most common mistake? Scanning too early. [pause 0.3]
> [PL-025b] If you look around, then watch the ball for three seconds, the picture is already out of date. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-025c] The best moment for your last look is just as the pass is played — when the ball is travelling and nobody can take it from you. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-025d] You can practise this alone. Play the ball against a wall, and every time it leaves your foot, turn your head and name one thing behind you. A tree. A fence. A colour. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-025e] It feels silly for a week. Then it becomes automatic. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-025f] Some coaches use a simple test: freeze a training game at random and ask a player, without looking, where the nearest opponent is. If he knows, he was scanning.

### 4:30 — ⏸ MID-ROLL — عادت ۲: نیم‌چرخش (2:15)
**صحنه‌ها:**
1. (4:30) کارت عنوان «HABIT 2 · THE HALF-TURN» با صدای گچ.
2. (4:40) زاویه‌ی بدن با نقاله‌ی گچی: بدن «بسته» (پشت به زمین حریف، ۰ درجه)، بدن «نیم‌باز» (حدود ۴۵ درجه).
3. (5:10) مخروط دید و گزینه‌های پاس برای هر حالت: حالت بسته ۱ گزینه (برگشت)، حالت نیم‌باز ۳ گزینه (برگشت، عرضی، رو به جلو).
4. (5:45) فوت‌ورک: پای عقب، لمس اول به جلو.
5. (6:20) جمع‌بندی.

> [PL-026] Habit two. Receive on the half-turn. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-027] Most players receive with their back to the game. Shoulders square to the passer, hips pointing back towards their own goal. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-028] From there, the only easy pass is the one that goes backwards. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-029] Now open the body. Not all the way — about halfway, so you can still see the passer and the pitch in front of you at the same time. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-030] Look at what changes. Back, sideways, forward. Three options instead of one. [pause 0.7]
> [PL-031] The trick is in the feet. Pedri lets the ball run across his body and takes it with the foot furthest from the pass. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-032] That one detail turns him the moment he touches it. No extra touch. No wasted second. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-033] If habit one tells you where to go, habit two lets you get there in one touch. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-033a] Watch out for the opposite mistake: opening up so much that you turn your back on the passer. [pause 0.3]
> [PL-033b] Now a heavy pass or a bad bounce catches you off guard, and the ball runs away from you. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-033c] Halfway is the sweet spot. You see the ball, you see the pitch, and your hips are already pointing where you want to go. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-033d] To practise it, put a cone two metres to your side. Every pass you receive, your first touch has to go past that cone — never back towards the wall.

### 6:45 — عادت ۳: پاس بعدی را آسان کن (2:00)
**صحنه‌ها:**
1. (6:45) کارت «HABIT 3 · THE NEXT PASS».
2. (6:55) زنجیره‌ی پاس شماره‌دار (۱ ← ۲ ← ۳) روی تخته. پاس اول به پایی که هم‌تیمی می‌خواهد و با سرعت درست.
3. (7:30) مقایسه: پاس «درست ولی سخت» (به پای اشتباه، کند) که هم‌تیمی را زیر فشار می‌گذارد، در مقابل پاس «آسان‌کننده».
4. (8:10) جمله‌ی کلیدی روی صفحه با تایپوگرافی بزرگ.

> [PL-034] Habit three is the one you won't find on a stats sheet. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-035] Make the next pass easy. [pause 0.8]
> [PL-036] A pass isn't finished when it arrives. It's finished when your teammate can do something with it. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-037] Watch this: the first pass is technically perfect — but it goes to his weaker foot, slowly, with an opponent closing. He survives, but only just. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-038] Now the same pass, played to his far foot, a little firmer, a little earlier. He doesn't even need to control it. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-039] That's what Pedri's teammates mean when they say playing with him is easy. He isn't just passing the ball. He's passing time. [pause 1.0]
> [PL-040] So before you pass, ask one question: which foot, and how fast? [pause 0.6]
> [PL-040a] The foot is usually the one furthest from the nearest opponent. [pause 0.3]
> [PL-040b] The speed depends on the pressure. If your teammate is free, a soft pass gives him time to look up. If he's marked, a firmer pass gets there before the defender does. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-040c] Notice that you can only make that choice if you scanned first. The three habits aren't separate tricks. They're one chain: [pause 0.4]
> [PL-040d] See early. Turn in one touch. Give your teammate an easier ball than the one you received.
> [PL-040e] Watch Pedri for ten minutes of any match and count how often his teammates take the ball in their stride. That is this habit, working quietly.

### 8:45 — تمرین ۱۰ دقیقه‌ای (1:15)
**تصویر:** دیاگرام تمرین متحرک: ۴ مخروط رنگی (آبی، اناری، طلایی، سفید) در مربع ۱۰ در ۱۰ متری. بازیکن وسط، پاس‌دهنده بیرون. یک نفر سوم (یا خود پاس‌دهنده) رنگی را پشت بازیکن نشان می‌دهد. تایمر ۱۰:۰۰ و سه مرحله‌ی ۳ دقیقه‌ای.

> [PL-041] Here's the drill. Four cones in a ten-metre square — four different colours. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-042] You stand in the middle. A friend passes to you from outside the square. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-043] Just before each pass, they point at one cone behind you. You have to scan, find it, receive on the half-turn, and play out through that side. [pause 0.6]
> [PL-044] Three minutes slow. Three minutes at match speed. Three minutes with one touch only. [pause 0.5]
> [PL-045] Ten minutes, three habits, every time you train.
> [PL-045a] Make it harder every week: shrink the square, add a second defender, or have your friend call the colour later and later. [pause 0.4]
> [PL-045b] The goal is not to look busy. It is to make your first look automatic, so that in a match you never have to think about it at all.
