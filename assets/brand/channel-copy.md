The Culer Files is an unofficial fan channel telling the stories and tactics that built FC Barcelona — explained on the chalkboard.

Every week:
• Full Stories — illustrated documentaries on Barça legends: Cruyff, Kubala, the Messi era
• The Match That… — the nights that changed the club, rebuilt move by move
• Play Like Barça — learn the habits of Pedri, Lamine Yamal and more
• What If? — transparent simulations of Barça's biggest crossroads

Unofficial fan channel. Not affiliated with FC Barcelona.
